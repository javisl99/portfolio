## Context

Airbus was my first meaningful professional experience inside the SAP ecosystem. I joined the project through Accenture to contribute to the build of a B2B site from scratch, with early exposure to both SAP Commerce Cloud and SAP ABAP.

This stage matters because it explains where my technical foundation comes from and why I was later able to grow with more judgment in more mature projects.

## What was at stake

The challenge here was not broad ownership yet, but foundation-building: learning quickly, delivering inside a real project, and starting to understand how a SAP Commerce solution is structured when there is no existing product to lean on.

## What I did

- Contributed as a back-end developer to the initial build of the B2B project.
- Started working with SAP Commerce Cloud and SAP ABAP inside a real professional context.
- Built the technical and functional foundation that later allowed me to move more confidently into support, customization, and production work.

## What this demonstrates

This case does not yet represent my highest-autonomy stage, but it leaves an important signal: my SAP Commerce profile starts from a real back-end foundation and from fast learning inside a demanding project from its earliest phase.
