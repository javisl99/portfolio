## Context

At Minsait I contributed to the Claro Perú project in a telco e-commerce setting built on SAP Commerce Cloud. This stage was especially valuable because it let me go much deeper into the platform standard and work on highly sensitive business flows.

It was a shorter engagement than other stages of my career, but a very useful one to show concrete technical depth.

## Role and responsibility

My work focused on understanding the SAP Commerce baseline and applying customizations in critical system areas without losing sight of how the standard behaves.

It was not only about isolated implementation work: the key was understanding where to change, how to change, and what consequences each decision could have on already sensitive processes.

## SAP technical focus

The main technical fronts were:

- checkout customizations,
- pricing-related logic,
- stock handling,
- and integration object generation.

This gave me a stronger view of how SAP Commerce can be extended in core e-commerce flows without turning every requirement into a break from the platform baseline.

## Decisions and customizations delivered

### Checkout as a critical flow

Working on checkout forced me to think in terms of continuity, functional impact, and sensitivity of change inside the purchase journey.

### Pricing and stock with real business context

Price and availability are not secondary details. They directly shape the buying experience, and this stage helped me treat those areas with stronger technical rigor.

### Integration objects as part of the SAP ecosystem

Working on integration objects expanded my understanding of the SAP ecosystem beyond the storefront or immediate business logic itself.

## Professional impact

This case matters because it shows something highly valued in SAP recruiting: understanding the standard, touching sensitive flows, and extending the platform with sound judgment instead of doing so indiscriminately.
