## Context

In Claro Perú I worked inside a telco e-commerce environment built on SAP Commerce Cloud, where several of the areas involved were directly tied to buying experience and overall platform consistency.

It was a more focused collaboration than other stages of my career, but especially valuable because it allowed me to go deeper into the standard and into flows that tend to be business-sensitive.

## What was at stake

Checkout, pricing, stock, and integration objects are not secondary concerns. They are areas where poorly judged changes can create functional problems, technical debt, or maintenance pain.

The real challenge was to intervene there with judgment, understanding when the standard should be extended and when it should not.

## What I did

- Worked on checkout-related customizations inside a real e-commerce context.
- Contributed to pricing and stock-related logic, gaining a clearer view of the functional impact behind each change.
- Collaborated on integration objects, expanding my understanding of the SAP ecosystem beyond storefront logic alone.
- Built stronger judgment around customizing core flows without turning every need into an unnecessary divergence from the platform standard.

## What this demonstrates

This case reinforces a very specific signal: I do not only implement changes. I understand the SAP Commerce standard more deeply and can work on sensitive flows with better technical rigor and stronger customization judgment.
