## Problem

BuildingCenter was not a contained development exercise. It was a live SAP Commerce Cloud platform with real operational pressure, business-sensitive flows, and direct visibility from the client side.

That meant every change had to balance delivery speed, platform stability, rigorous incident investigation, and a clear understanding of functional impact.

## Solution

- Built backend functionality using Java, Spring, and SAP Commerce Cloud on top of an already active production platform.
- Worked on services, data models, jobs, interceptors, validators, integrations, and business logic tied to core platform behavior.
- Investigated incidents, traced root causes, and helped improve platform stability instead of treating tickets as isolated issues.
- Combined Java, Spring, and SQL to diagnose complex problems and used AI-Assisted debugging to accelerate analysis and narrow hypotheses faster.
- Collaborated directly with business stakeholders, QA, and functional teams to turn requirements and incidents into scalable technical actions.
- Used AI-Assisted workflows to accelerate technical analysis, debugging, documentation, and repetitive engineering tasks while keeping final technical judgment in my hands.

## Tech stack

Java, Spring, SAP Commerce Cloud, SQL, FlexibleSearch, enterprise integrations, incident analysis, and AI-Assisted development workflows.

## Impact

This case best reflects my current positioning as a Backend Software Engineer: someone able to contribute to feature delivery, sustain production, investigate complex issues, improve platform stability, and communicate effectively across technical and business-facing layers of an enterprise platform.
