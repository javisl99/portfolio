## Context

BuildingCenter is the case that best reflects my current profile. At Stratesys I worked on this project with CaixaBank Tech in a live SAP Commerce Cloud environment where production support, functional continuity, and client visibility were all part of daily delivery.

The experience had two clear phases: an earlier stage more centered on incidents and a later return with stronger technical grounding and much more autonomy inside the same project.

## What was at stake

This was not just about closing tickets. It meant keeping a real platform stable, understanding the business impact behind each incident, and giving the client a clear view of status, risk, and sensible next steps.

In that kind of context, value does not come only from solving fast. It comes from solving with judgment, context, and continuity.

## What I did

- Handled incidents in SAP Commerce Cloud with ongoing technical follow-up and direct client visibility.
- Grew from a more support-oriented role into owning the incident stream inside the project.
- Contributed not only to corrective support, but also to enhancement work on the platform.
- Built stronger functional context so I could connect debugging, business impact, and technical decisions.

## What this demonstrates

This case leaves a very clear signal for recruiters and technical reviewers: I can work on a SAP Commerce platform in production, take ownership of the incident stream, and operate comfortably across support, continuous change delivery, and client-facing technical communication.
