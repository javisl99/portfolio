## Context

BuildingCenter was not a contained development exercise. It was a live SAP Commerce Cloud platform with real operational pressure, production support, business-sensitive flows, and direct visibility from the client side.

That meant backend work had to coexist with incidents, existing integrations, functional constraints, and continuous delivery needs.

## Problem

The challenge was not only to build functionality. The challenge was to do it without losing stability in a platform already running real operations, and to investigate incidents deeply enough to solve the actual problem instead of just closing tickets.

## What I did

- Built backend functionality with Java, Spring, and SAP Commerce Cloud on top of an already active production platform.
- Worked on services, data models, jobs, interceptors, validators, integrations, and business logic tied to core platform behavior.
- Investigated incidents, traced root causes, and contributed to stability improvements instead of treating tickets as isolated issues.
- Combined Java, Spring, and SQL to diagnose complex problems and reduce analysis time.
- Collaborated with client, QA, and functional teams to turn incidents and requirements into concrete technical actions.
- Used AI selectively to accelerate context reading, technical documentation, and early hypothesis checking.

## Technical stack

Java, Spring, SAP Commerce Cloud, SQL, FlexibleSearch, integrations, incident analysis, jobs, interceptors, and validators.

## Impact

This case reflects the kind of backend work I do best today: shipping features on a live platform, sustaining production, solving incidents with incomplete information, and collaborating with business-facing teams without losing technical focus.

## What this shows about my profile

It shows a backend profile that does more than implement features. I can also work on production support, integrations, and technical decisions inside a business platform that is already running.
