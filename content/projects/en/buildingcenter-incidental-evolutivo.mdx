## Context

At Stratesys I worked on the BuildingCenter project together with CaixaBank Tech. It is the case that best represents my professional growth inside SAP Commerce Cloud because it combines production support, direct client interaction, and gradual exposure to evolutive work.

The experience was not linear: there was a first stage focused mainly on incidents and a second stage where I returned with a stronger technical base and broader responsibility inside the same project.

## Role and responsibility

My starting point was resolving incidents and keeping the client informed about the state of each case. That mattered because it forced me to understand not only the technical issue itself, but also the real impact of each incident and the need to communicate clearly.

In the second stage I took full ownership of the incident stream and started contributing to evolutive work as well, which raised the level of responsibility and required me to combine support, technical judgment, and project continuity.

## SAP technical focus

This case reflects three particularly valuable areas:

- diagnosing and resolving incidents in SAP Commerce Cloud,
- maintaining a live platform while working directly with the client,
- and growing from pure support into evolutive delivery on top of the existing base.

More than a case about one isolated feature, it is a case about technical sustainability and ownership inside a real SAP environment.

## Decisions and work delivered

### Support with visibility for the client

A key part of the work was not only solving the issue technically, but also keeping the client updated on status, progress, and the most reasonable next step.

### Moving from support to broader technical responsibility

As my project knowledge improved, I evolved from a more support-oriented role to full incident ownership and then into evolutive participation as well.

### Understanding the project as a system, not as isolated tickets

The real improvement came from connecting incidents, functional context, and technical decisions instead of treating every case as something fully disconnected.

## Professional impact

This case is one of the strongest early signals of seniority in my path: it shows growth in autonomy, support judgment, client communication, and the ability to technically sustain a SAP Commerce project already running in production.
