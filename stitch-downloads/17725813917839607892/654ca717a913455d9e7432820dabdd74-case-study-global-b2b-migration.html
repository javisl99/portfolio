The Context

TechCorp's flagship B2B platform, serving a global retailer with $2B in annual digital revenue, was operating on a decade-old SAP Hybris monolith. The system was fragile, release cycles spanned 8 weeks, and the maintenance of synchronous ERP integrations caused frequent checkout failures during peak holiday traffic.

What was at Stake

warning

Technical Debt

A tightly coupled architecture meant a failure in the loyalty service could crash the entire storefront, leading to millions in lost potential revenue.

trending_down

Business Velocity

Market competitors were pivoting to headless commerce. Our client couldn't launch new regional storefronts faster than 6 months.

What I Did

1

Architectural Strategy

Designed a Strangler Fig pattern migration path. We decoupled the core cart and checkout logic into Spring Boot microservices while keeping the legacy SAP system as the "source of record" for ERP sync.

2

Event-Driven Integration

Implemented Kafka for asynchronous order processing. This isolated the frontend from legacy ERP latency, ensuring customers could always complete orders even if downstream systems were slow.

3

DevOps Leadership

Introduced CI/CD pipelines via Azure DevOps and containerized the suite with Kubernetes, reducing deployment time from 4 hours of manual labor to 12 minutes automated.
